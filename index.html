02 / A LITTLE UNDERSTANDING

The science behind
the movement.

You don’t have to memorize the universe.
Start with a few ideas you can see.

01 — FREE FALL↓

Falling is
accelerating.

Gravity changes velocity by the same amount each second. Without air resistance, objects dropped from the same height land together, whatever their mass.

v = gt speed from rest, before impact
02 — PROJECTILES⌒

One path.
Two motions.

Horizontal velocity stays steady while gravity changes vertical velocity. Together they trace a parabola. A 45° launch travels farthest when both ends are at the same height.

R = v₀² sin(2θ) / g range at equal launch & landing heights
03 — ORBITS◌

Falling, all the
way around.

An orbit is continuous free fall. A satellite moves sideways fast enough to keep missing Earth. Gravity changes its direction, even when its speed stays constant.

v = √(GM / r) circular orbit; r is measured from Earth’s center
One more thing: mass and weight aren’t the same.＋

Mass measures how much matter an object has, in kilograms. Weight is the gravitational force on it, in newtons: W = mg. A 2 kg object has the same mass on Earth and the Moon, but weighs about 19.62 N on Earth and 3.24 N on the Moon.
